Created with the built-in image generation tool and exported as a JPEG for the website. The page animates the photograph with a slow zoom and drifting mist; it does not download a video. Visitors can pause the motion. Motion starts paused when their device requests reduced motion.

Wide landscape 16:9 composition with a central vanishing point, distant details softened naturally by fog, subtle texture in foreground. Lighting/mood: peaceful overcast dawn, luminous mist, muted sage, slate and cool grey tones, low contrast, photographic realism. No people, no vehicles, no text, no signs, no logos, no watermark. The road should feel inviting and calm rather than eerie. This is a clean background asset, not a UI mockup.
